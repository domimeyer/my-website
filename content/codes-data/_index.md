My research relies on numerical simulations, post-processing tools and data analysis methods to study massive stars, their circumstellar environments, supernova remnants, pulsars and pulsar wind nebulae.

This page gathers selected information about numerical methods, codes, data products and reproducibility material related to my scientific work.

## Numerical codes 


I use publicly available numerical codes and software tools for my research, mainly for hydrodynamical and magnetohydrodynamical simulations, radiative-transfer post-processing, radio/sub-mm data analysis, non-thermal emission modelling and the analysis of large numerical datasets.
